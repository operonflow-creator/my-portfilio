AI Workflow Automation

Intelligent workflows that combine business logic with AI decision-making to handle end-to-end processes.

Example: Incoming documents classified by an LLM, routed for approval, and filed automatically.

AI Agents & LLM Systems

Custom agents built on OpenAI, Claude, and other LLM APIs that reason, use tools, and take action.

Example: A research agent that gathers data, summarizes findings, and posts a briefing to Slack.

n8n Automation Development

Production-grade n8n workflows with error handling, retries, logging, and clean architecture.

Example: A multi-branch n8n workflow syncing orders between an e-commerce platform, sheets, and email.

Web Scraping & Data Pipelines

Python-based scrapers and ETL pipelines that collect, clean, and deliver structured data reliably.

Example: Daily competitor pricing collected, normalized, and delivered to a dashboard.

API & SaaS Integrations

Connect the tools you already use — CRMs, email, sheets, databases — with REST APIs, OAuth, and webhooks.

Example: New CRM deals automatically create invoices, folders, and kickoff notifications.

RAG & Knowledge Systems

Retrieval-augmented generation systems that let AI answer questions from your own documentation and data.

Example: An internal assistant that answers policy questions from your company wiki with citations.
